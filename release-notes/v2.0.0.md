## What's new

- **Build your own scenery packages.** OrthoStreamXP can now make photo scenery packages for any part of the world from X-Plane's own Global Scenery, right on your PC. You no longer need to download a whole region's mesh, or a separate tool such as Ortho4XP. Open the **Packages** page: its first tab, **Build own packages**, shows a map of the world. Pick the areas you fly, click **Build the picked tiles**, and each 1° tile is ready in a few seconds.
- **Pick on a map, or by name.** Click a tile to pick or drop it, Shift+drag to pick a rectangle, and Ctrl+drag to drop one. Or type a continent, country, region or state under **Pick by region or country**, such as *Germany*, *Bavaria* or *Western Europe*, and click **Pick**: every tile it touches that X-Plane has scenery for is picked. The map shows which tiles are picked, built, building or failed, with the airports, the cities and OpenStreetMap underneath.
- **X-Plane's own ground, with photos on it.** Each tile keeps X-Plane's relief, water and coastlines. Its ground gets the photo textures, with finer photos around the runways. X-Plane's roads, forests and buildings go into an overlay package beside it, so towns still have their buildings, and **Night lights** still light the streets.
- **The first setup builds them too.** In the setup's **Scenery** step, **No scenery packages yet?** now offers **Build your own packages…**. The folder they are built in becomes your scenery packages folder.
- **Downloading a region is still there.** The Packages page's second tab, **Download kubilus1 packages**, has the region downloads from before, unchanged.

## How building works

The packages are made from the Global Scenery already installed with your X-Plane. It is only read, never changed. Building needs no download: the photos are streamed as you fly, as with any other package. The tiles you can pick are the ones your X-Plane has scenery for, including its Demo Areas.

The packages are named after their continent, such as **OrthoGlobal_Europe_XP** or **OrthoGlobal_NorthAmerica_XP**, so you can tell them from the downloaded regions (**OrthoStream_Eur**...). The roads, forests and buildings of all of them go into **OrthoGlobal_Overlays_XP**. They are built in the folder shown under **Scenery packages folder** at the top of the tab. That is your scenery packages folder unless you choose another with **Choose…**. If the tab warns that OrthoStreamXP will not stream the packages built there, click **Stream from here** to make that folder your scenery packages folder.

Building again skips the tiles that are already up to date, so you can add areas later at any time. **Stop** ends a build part-way, and the tiles already built are kept. The page remembers the tiles you picked for the next start.

## Good to know

- **Keep the built packages in a folder of their own.** Don't build them into a folder that already holds downloaded regions or packages from Ortho4XP, AutoOrtho or another tool. Two packages for the same area bring two meshes, and the roads and buildings can show twice. Click **Choose…** and pick a new, empty folder, then click **Stream from here**.
- **Stream from here** works while OrthoStreamXP is stopped. You can build while it runs, but the new tiles are streamed only from its next start. Stop and start OrthoStreamXP, with X-Plane closed, to use them.
- If you close the window during a build, OrthoStreamXP asks first. The tiles already built are kept.
- The map's **OpenStreetMap** background is downloaded as you look at it and kept for two weeks. Untick **OpenStreetMap** if you don't want it.
- The packages are made from your own X-Plane installation and are not for redistribution.
- Nothing changes for your existing packages or settings with the update. Packages you downloaded or made before work as they did.
